# Pink Beach: Labuan Bajo’s Rosy Retreat

Dawn stroll on blush sands, snorkel vibrant reefs & hunt pink shells with Kapalsantai.

A Blush on the Horizon
Pantai Pink, just a 90-minute speedboat from Labuan Bajo, is one of only seven pink
beaches worldwide—its blush hue comes from crushed red coral and foraminifera shells. In
1–2 m crystal-clear shallows, snorkel among clownfish, parrotfish, and baby turtles. There
are no facilities, so pack water, snacks, and sun protection. Arrive at dawn for the sand’s
rosy peak, then kayak or lounge on the soft pastel grains. Ready to paint your own
adventure pink?


3 Rosy Revelations of Pantai Pink
   1. Coral & Shell Origins
       Tiny red-coral fragments and foraminifera shells crushed over centuries create the
       pink hue.
   2. Seasonal Peak
       Visit in the dry months (June - September) when lower tides reveal more rosy sand
   3. Turtle Nursery Nearby
       Seagrass beds offshore nurture baby green turtles, keep an eye out during your dip.


Why Pink Beach Belongs on Your Bucket List
   1. Global Rarity
       Only a handful of beaches share this rosy glow—Indonesia’s own natural marvel.
   2. Snorkeler’s Paradise
       Shallow reefs alive with vibrant corals and fish just a few strokes from shore.
   3. Photo-Ready Scenery
       Volcanic islets, turquoise sea, and a blush-pink shoreline—no filter needed.


3 Must-Do Experiences at Pink Beach
   1. Dawn Stroll on Rose-Tinted Sand
       Arrive by 06:00 to experience the sand at its pinkest. A lightweight sarong doubles as
       a comfy picnic blanket while the island wakes up.
   2. Snorkel the Color Parade
       Wade into waist-deep water off the east end. Look for clownfish in anemones,
       parrotfish grazing on coral, and the occasional green turtle drifting by
   3. Pink-Tinted Shell Hunt
       Trace the high-tide line just after sunrise: spot blush coral chips, tiny spiral shells, and
       rosy scallop shards—snap a photo, then leave them to keep the sand glowing for the
       next explorer.

Best Season to Visit
    ● June- September: Pantai Pink is at its brightest pink sands


What to Pack
    ●   Quick-dry swimwear & rash guard
    ●   Sunscreen & wide-brimmed hat
    ●   Dry bag or waterproof phone case
    ●   Water & light snacks
    ●   Beach towel & change of clothes
