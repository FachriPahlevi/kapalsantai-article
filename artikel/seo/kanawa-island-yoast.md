- Meta description: **Plan your Kanawa Island visit from Labuan Bajo. Explore snorkeling, beach time, viewpoints, boat access, and what to bring.**
- Social title: **Kanawa Island: A Peaceful Coast Near Labuan Bajo**
- Social description: **Discover clear water, beach views, snorkeling, and practical tips for a Kanawa Island visit.**
- Alt foto udara: **Aerial view of an island, beach, jetty and surrounding reef**.
- Alt foto pantai: **A person relaxing on a beach while two people enjoy the water**.
- Schema: gunakan pengaturan default Yoast yang sesuai jenis halaman; cek agar tidak duplikat.
- Periksa lokasi aktual dan izin kedua foto, waktu perjalanan, akses, tautan serta ketepatan informasi sebelum publikasi.
